- In code/s81-showdialog.csv (getidsofnames row), tool/s81_showdialog.py and s85-submission.md, the five-position sequence omits riid. 0x1345/134c put ShowDialog (0x7820) in the name array at [rsp+D0]. 0x1357/136a put GetUserDefaultLCID() into [rsp+20]. 0x135d/1365 put &[rsp+C8] into [rsp+28]. 0x136e/1374 set R9D=1, 0x1377 points R8 at the name array, 0x137f points RDX at 16 zero bytes (0x82F8), 0x1386 sets RCX=RDI. Consequently 0x1389 calls GetIDsOfNames(this, IID_NULL, names, 1, lcid, &dispid). The sixth parameter is at [rsp+28]. The supplied native.c already shows all six. On Invoke at 0x1444, the listed stack values are correct and occupy positions 5 through 9 inclusive. Proposed regression must compare all six producers individually, then substitute the submitted shifted tuple as the negative control. See https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/api/oaidl/nf-oaidl-idispatch-getidsofnames.
- 0x13a7 packs cArgs=1, cNamedArgs=0. 0x13c2/13cb produce element stride 24 before OLEAUT32 VariantInit at 0x13cf. The public contract only guarantees vt=VT_EMPTY. The submitted claim that 24 bytes were zeroed is unsupported. The original explicitly writes VT_BSTR at 0x13e2 and the incoming RCX (preserved in RBP at 0x1335) into the union at 0x13e7. This routine does not establish allocation of that BSTR. At 0x1449 ESI takes the low 32 payload bits on success, followed by Release at 0x1457 and VariantClear(result) at 0x1463. See https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/api/oleauto/nf-oleauto-variantinit.
